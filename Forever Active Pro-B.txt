Support your body like never before with the latest in scientific advancements and new, clinically studied probiotic strains. Designed to promote a healthy digestive system, Forever Active Pro-B® includes FloraActive—a range of beneficial probiotic strains from Copenhagen University Hospital, a leader in the gastroenterology field.

With a proprietary blend of 6 different strains, Forever Active Pro-B® probiotic provides 8 billion CFU per dose. These strains were researched and selected based on their beneficial properties, clinical research and their ability to work synergistically. This formula also includes a prebiotic fiber to sustain the probiotics during storage, transport and delivery to the gut.

Prebiotics provide the ideal environment for the probiotic to function and thrive. The prebiotic fiber selected is a short chain fructo-oligosaccharide that is known to support fermentation of bifidobacteria and lactobacillus species used in the Forever Active Pro-B® formula.
