Description
Refresh your routine with the potential benefits of pure inner leaf aloe vera gel featuring a natural, sugar-free lemon-lime flavor. 

With 96% inner leaf aloe vera gel, Forever Aloe Vera Juice helps support gut and immune health while helping maintain natural energy.

Forever’s aloe vera gel is certified by the International Aloe Science Council and backed by a team of scientific experts to ensure quality at every step.

It all begins in our fields, where we grow an average of 40 million aloe vera plants each year. We use only Aloe barbadensis Miller, the most nutritionally valuable variety.

Once the leaves reach full maturity, they are carefully hand-harvested and hand-filleted to remove the pure inner leaf aloe vera gel. Soon after, the gel is aseptically processed using Forever’s proprietary method to preserve freshness and potency.

To complete this commitment to quality and conscious manufacturing, Forever Aloe Vera Juice is packaged in a 100% recyclable PET bottle made with 50% post-consumer recycled content.
