Forever’s Aloe Body Lotion is the perfect everyday moisturizer to soften skin and leave it feeling smooth, hydrated and healthy. Our lightweight formula absorbs quickly to deliver the benefits of pure aloe vera and other moisturizing ingredients almost instantly.

We combined a high concentration of pure inner leaf aloe vera with plant-based oils and botanicals to put nature’s most powerful skin enhancing ingredients to work for you. Aloe forms the ideal base for our formula by moisturizing and promoting healthy, youthful looking skin. Argan oil contains vitamin E and fatty acids that help soften skin while hydrolyzed jojoba esters help promote appearance of firmness and skin elasticity.

The addition of macadamia seed oil makes Aloe Body Lotion an ideal choice for dry and mature skin due to its high palmitoleic acid content, which promotes healthy looking skin. But this formula isn’t just for people with dry or mature skin. Forever created this silicone-free formula for anyone with normal to dry skin by adding ingredients like sodium hyaluronate, which forms a protective barrier on the skin and helps retain moisture. Aloe Body Lotion also features a bright aroma featuring light florals for a scent that’s universally appealing.

When you need a go-to daily lotion that leaves skin feeling soft and silky, but never greasy, make sure you have Forever’s Aloe Body Lotion close by.
Usage
Apply liberally and gently massage until lotion is completely absorbed into the skin.
