Description
Moisturize and soothe your skin after a long day or a challenging workout with Aloe Cooling Lotion. This rich yet light formula provides a cooling sensation with an invigorating mint scent.

Aloe Cooling Lotion imparts a soothing feeling to the skin with uplifting, energizing menthol. Eucalyptus oil, camphor and rosemary extract add to the invigorating effect. The addition of Methylsulfonyl Methane, or MSM — an organic compound known as the “beauty mineral” — may help improve post-workout discomfort.

This formula also refreshes the skin and absorbs quickly without feeling greasy. Inner leaf aloe provides skin-supporting nutrients such as vitamins A, C and E, and hyaluronic acid helps support a healthy skin texture.

Cool, soothe and nourish your skin with the power of menthol, inner leaf aloe, and other natural ingredients. Support your active lifestyle and experience a relaxed sensation by massaging your skin with refreshing Aloe Cooling Lotion.
