Description
Apply this restorative cream when you need extra hydration and let the deep moisturization revitalize your skin. Deep moisturizing cream elevates your skin care routine by quenching your skin’s thirst with aloe, ceramides and moisturizing botanicals.


There’s nothing like pure inner leaf aloe vera gel from Forever’s own fields to provide nourishing natural moisture to your skin. Ceramides complement the powerful effects of aloe vera perfectly by providing restoring properties to improve the appearance of skin’s texture and suppleness.


But the revitalizing benefits don’t stop there. We also included pine bark extract, which contains antioxidants that fight against environmental stressors, help your skin preserve collagen, and provide a youthful appearance. Another powerful ingredient, palmitoyl tripeptide-5, is a wrinkle-smoothing peptide that helps improve the appearance of skin texture.


To get the most out of this hydrating cream, use daily in the morning and/or at night for an intense deep moisturizing effect.

Usage
Use daily for an intense deep moisturizing effect. Apply a generous amount of cream to the face using fingertips, then massage into skin.
