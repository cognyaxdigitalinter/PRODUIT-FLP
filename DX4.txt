Breakfast: Take 3 Forever Therm Plus™ tablets with 8 fl. oz. of water.

Mid-morning: Take 2 Forever DuoPure™ tablets with 8 fl. oz. of water.Take 8 Forever Sensatiable™ chewable tablets at any time throughout the day with 8 fl. oz. of water.

Afternoon: Mix 1 scoop of Forever Plant Protein™ with 8 fl. oz. of unsweetened plant-based milk or water.

Mid-Afternoon: Dissolve 1 Forever Multi Fizz™ effervescent tablet in a 10 fl. oz. glass of water. Also, drink 330 mL. of Forever Aloe Vera Gel®.

Evening (or before bed): Mix 1 scoop of Forever Plant Protein™ with 8 fl. oz. of unsweetened plant-based milk or water. Also, pour contents of 1 Forever LemonBlast™packet into 8 fl. oz. of cool water and mix thoroughly.
