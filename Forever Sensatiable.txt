Description
Feel full longer with Forever Sensatiable®, a chewable tablet that can be taken on the go to stop cravings in their tracks. The lemon flavor is naturally sweetened with dry honey powder and reminiscent of a lemon-cream cookie. 

 
Forever Sensatiable® promotes feelings of satiety using oats and a clinically studied yeast hydrolysate. Oats contain beta-glucan, a natural source of soluble fiber, which slows food transit in the digestive tract, contributing to a steadier blood sugar and supporting a healthy cholesterol level. The yeast hydrolysate is derived from protein hydrolysis of a yeast species that has been used for thousands of years in baking, fermented food and beverage manufacturing processes. It has been shown to impact two appetite regulation hormones, ghrelin and leptin, which may promote a reduction in caloric intake and weight loss from fat mass reduction. 

 
Forever Sensatiable® also contains glucomannan, which is extracted from konjac root, or elephant yam. This dietary fiber absorbs water in the stomach to contribute to feelings of satiety, which may also support reduced calorie intake and reduced absorption of fat. 

 
Additionally, medium chain triglycerides may increase the release of appetite hormones that promote the feeling of fullness in the body. They may also help with storing less fat in the body and raising the amount of energy used by muscles. 

 
Whether you’re looking to maintain or lose weight, or just want a little something between meals, reach for easy-to-take Forever Sensatiable® chewable tablets and drink plenty of water to help curb your hunger throughout the day.
