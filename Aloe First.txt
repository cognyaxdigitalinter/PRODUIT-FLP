Description
Soothe and support skin comfort with Aloe First®. Aloe has long been used to soothe and provide moisture relief to dry, normal and combination skin.  
 
But the benefits don’t stop there. Aloe First also contains bee propolis to elevate the natural soothing properties of aloe vera and as well as allantoin to help condition and comfort the skin.  
 
Aloe Barbadensis Leaf Juice 
Helps moisturize and soften the skin while providing hydration. 
 
Propolis Extract 
Helps soothe the skin and provides skin-conditioning benefits.  
 
Allantoin 
Helps soothe the skin and promotes smoother-looking skin.  

The combination of these ingredients plus a blend of 11 botanical extracts – including chamomile, eucalyptus, sage and sandalwood extracts – makes this soothing mist great for use on skin exposed to environmental stressors.  
 
This versatile, pH-balanced spray provides a gentle mist for quick hydration. It’s easy to apply and gets to work fast, making it a useful addition whenever you need to experience the soothing power of aloe vera!
