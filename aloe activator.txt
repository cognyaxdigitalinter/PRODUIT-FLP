Usage
Aloe activator is a multi-purpose product to clean or freshen, apply to a cotton ball and gently wipe across skin. For an Aloe Mask, mix 1 tsp mask powder with 1 tsp aloe activator and apply a thin layer to clean skin. After 20 minutes, rinse thoroughly with warm water. Use 2-3 times weekly.
