Description
Make a natural, youthful glow part of your morning routine with Forever Vitamin C. This brightening facial milk delivers a potent 6% concentration of clinically tested vitamin C in a silky texture that melts into skin. 

Designed for daily use, Forever Vitamin C supports the appearance of brighter skin and helps even skin tone — plus it features nourishing ingredients like aloe vera and jojoba. 
 
Tetrahexyldecyl Ascorbate (Vitamin C)  
A highly stable and fat-soluble form of vitamin C that penetrates further into the skin and helps even skin tone, minimizes the appearance of dark spots, and brightens the complexion. 
 
Pure Inner Leaf Aloe Vera Gel  
Provides a moisturizing effect that supports the feeling of healthy-looking skin. It’s harvested by hand and aseptically processed using Forever’s proprietary method to preserve freshness and potency.   
  
Hydrolyzed Jojoba Esters 
Helps maintain the skin’s natural moisture barrier and helps keep skin feeling nourished and hydrated. 
 
Featuring a clean, vegan formula, Forever Vitamin C is suitable for all skin types and has no added fragrance, parabens, phthalates or sulfates. It pairs perfectly with Forever Bakuchiol™, a nighttime facial milk that promotes the appearance of renewed skin. Additionally, it complements Forever’s other skin care products, integrating seamlessly into your routine.
