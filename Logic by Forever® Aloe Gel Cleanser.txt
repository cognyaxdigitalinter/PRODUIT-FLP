Description
Experience a blend of purity and performance with Logic by Forever® aloe gel cleanser, a gentle yet effective cleanser for all skin types, including sensitive skin and combination skin. The cleanser’s plant-powered formula utilizes an advanced gel-based technology that provides an encapsulation system, keeping botanicals together to deliver ingredients to the skin. 
 
Pure Inner Leaf Aloe Vera Gel 
Our hero ingredient, and first in the formula, provides a moisturizing effect that supports the feeling of healthy-looking skin. It’s harvested by hand from our own fields and aseptically processed within six hours using Forever’s proprietary method to preserve freshness and potency. 
 
Hydrolyzed Jojoba Esters  
Helps skin retain moisture and supports the skin’s moisture barrier while promoting gentle scrubbing for cleansed skin. 
 
Desert Date Fruit Extract  
Features vitamins and minerals and helps promote skin hydration. 
 
Featuring naturally derived cleansers, Logic by Forever® aloe gel cleanser provides a luxurious, sulfate-free lather that cleanses skin. Plus, it has no added fragrance, parabens, phthalates, or sulfates — resulting in a cleanser you can feel good about using.
