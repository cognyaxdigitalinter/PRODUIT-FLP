Forever’s Aloe MSM Gel is your ticket to soothing relief when and where you need it. We combined pure inner leaf aloe vera with methyl sulfonyl methane (MSM), an organic sulfur found in many living organisms. MSM has powerful skin calming properties and combined with pure aloe vera gel provides deep comfort and moisturization.

But the soothing power of Aloe MSM Gel doesn’t stop with those two ingredients. This clear, non-staining formula includes natural herbal extracts like tea tree leaf oil, willow bark extract and glycerin to calm and condition skin. The addition of rosemary leaf extract draws water to the skin to keep it hydrated and comfortable.

This versatile gel is a must-have for any gym bag. Apply liberally and massage into skin to soothe sore joints and muscles after a tough workout. If your lifestyle keeps you on the move, show your body the love it deserves and stay ready for the next adventure.
