Designed for nightly use, Forever Bakuchiol helps reduce the appearance of fine lines and wrinkles and promotes the appearance of renewed skin. With a silky texture that melts into skin, Forever Bakuchiol also features nourishing ingredients like aloe vera and jojoba.
